# Logo Upload Instructions

## Upload Your HeavenCraft Logo Here

Please upload your logo image file to this folder with one of these names:
- `heavencraft-logo.png` (recommended)
- `heavencraft-logo.jpg`
- `heavencraft-logo.svg`

**Recommended specifications:**
- Format: PNG with transparent background (preferred) or SVG
- Size: At least 200px width
- Aspect ratio: Maintain your brand's aspect ratio

Once you upload the logo, the Header component will automatically display it.
